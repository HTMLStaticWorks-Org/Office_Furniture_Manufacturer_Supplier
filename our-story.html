WHO WE ARE

Built around the science of better workspaces.

Forma & Frame is a commercial office furniture manufacturer and supplier focused on ergonomic engineering and long-term corporate partnerships.

We bring industrial designers, CNC fabrication specialists and workspace planners together to build furniture that supports posture, scales across floors and reflects the culture of the organizations we equip. We value transparent timelines, precision engineering and consistency in every module.
