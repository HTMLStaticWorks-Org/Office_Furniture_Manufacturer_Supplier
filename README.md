# Forma & Frame — Office Furniture Manufacturers & Suppliers

A premium, architectural commercial office furniture manufacturing and bulk workspace supply website.

---

## Overview

Forma & Frame is an editorial, responsive multi-page website tailored for commercial office furniture manufacturers, workspace engineering specialists, and bulk B2B supply partners.

### Pages Included
- **Home 1 (`index.html`)**: Editorial sequence hero featuring modern workspaces, engineering highlights, workspace environments, plant statistics, architectural blueprint specification stage, and direct quote request.
- **Home 2 (`home-2.html`)**: Workspace studio line-up, "The Edit" interactive 3D-tilt cards showcase, engineering focus, 4-phase turnkey delivery pipeline, and corporate partnership inquiries.
- **Our Story (`our-story.html`)**: Manufacturing heritage, guiding engineering values, factory insights, and company timeline.
- **Solutions (`solutions.html`)**: 6-step workspace and manufacturing capabilities (Layout Planning, Material Curation, CNC Fabrication, Mock-up Sampling, Delivery & Site Install, OEM & Turnkey Support).
- **Furniture (`furniture.html`)**: Office furniture categorized by type (Ergonomic Task Chairs, Modular Workstations, Executive Desks & Suites, Conference & Boardroom, Lounge & Acoustic Pods, Storage & Lockers), material swatches, and interactive quote shortlist cart.
- **Bulk Orders (`bulk-orders.html`)**: B2B corporate rollout capabilities, turnkey contractor & dealer tiers, and volume production workflows.
- **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Showroom & factory visit information, general contact form, interactive map, and FAQ accordion.
- **Get a Quote (`get-quote.html`)**: Dedicated quote builder & BOQ specification request page linked from the header "Get a quote" CTA and quote buttons.

---

## Features & Technical Highlights

- **Architectural Theme Palette**: Deep Forest-Teal (`#1b5e57`), Luminous Sea-Teal (`#2e857c`), and Crisp Alabaster Stone (`#f6f8f7`) header/footer surfaces with full Dark Theme (`#111817` / `#52b7ae`) support.
- **Interactive Controls**: Bespoke SVG Light / Dark theme switcher and RTL (Right-to-Left) layout toggles in both navbar and mobile hamburger drawer.
- **Responsive Architecture**: Fully optimized across desktop (1440px+), laptop (1024px), tablet (768px), and mobile (320px–480px).
- **Smooth Animations**: CSS keyframe transitions, sequence reveals, and reduced-motion accessibility support.
