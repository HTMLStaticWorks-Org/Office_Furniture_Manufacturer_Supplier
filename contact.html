GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do you manufacture custom workstation dimensions and finishes?

Yes. We engineer modular workstations, conference tables and storage units tailored to your CAD floorplan, laminate swatches, cable management needs and corporate branding.

Can I request a volume quote for bulk or multi-floor office orders?

Absolutely. Visit our Get a Quote page to select your furniture categories, target quantities and installation window.

Can you set up a sample chair or mock-up workstation bay before production?

Yes. For corporate fit-outs and bulk orders, we provide physical material swatches, sample ergonomic seating and pilot workstation bays for sign-off prior to batch manufacturing.

What information should I share for an accurate BOQ quotation?

Share your furniture categories or architectural BOQ, workstation dimensions, finish preferences, estimated quantities, delivery city and target site readiness date.

Do you handle delivery and on-site installation?

Yes. We coordinate protective zone-coded flat-pack dispatch and deploy trained installation technicians for multi-floor corporate handovers.
